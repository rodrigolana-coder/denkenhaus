The British Royal Family
The United Kingdom is a monarchy, and today the country has a king.
But the king is not the head of the government.
The British monarch is the head of state, while the Prime Minister is the head of government.
The British monarchy is very old.
For hundreds of years, kings and queens had much more political power than they have today.
Today, the UK is a constitutional monarchy, which means that the monarch has an important official and ceremonial role, but does not govern the country.
The Royal Family is also an important part of British culture.
Royal weddings, birthdays and ceremonies can attract millions of people around the world.
The monarch's official London residence is Buckingham Palace.
It has hundreds of rooms and is one of London's most famous tourist attractions.
One famous tradition at the palace is the Changing of the Guard.
During the ceremony, one group of guards replaces another group.
Many tourists visit the palace to watch it.
The British monarchy also has a very long history.
Queen Elizabeth II was queen for more than 70 years.
She was born in 1926 and became queen in 1952.
She died in 2022 at the age of 96.
After her death, her son Charles became King Charles III.
So, although the monarch doesn't govern the UK, the Royal Family continues to be an important symbol of British history and tradition.
