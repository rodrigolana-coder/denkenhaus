Dialogues
Dialogue 1 — A Big Change
Emma What did your aunt do before she became a manager?
Lucas She worked in a small company.
Emma When did she become a manager?
Lucas She became a manager in 2015.
Emma Did she earn more money after that?
Lucas Yes, she did. She got a much better salary.
Emma What did she do with the money?
Lucas She bought a house and saved the rest.
Dialogue 2 — An Expensive Car
Anna Did your brother buy a new car?
John Yes, he did.
Anna How much did it cost?
John It cost twenty-eight thousand pounds.
Anna Wow! Where did he get the money?
John He sold his old car and saved money for three years.
Anna Did he spend all his savings?
John Almost!
Dialogue 3 — A Successful Business
Sophie When did your parents start their business?
Daniel They started it in 2008.
Sophie Did they make much money at first?
Daniel No, they didn't. The first year was difficult.
Sophie What happened later?
Daniel They found more customers and the business grew.
Sophie Did they become successful?
Daniel Yes. Last year, they sold more than 300,000 products.

Grammar — Past Simple: Irregular Verbs

1. Irregular Verbs

Some verbs do not use -ed in the Past Simple.

Their form changes.

2. Regular × Irregular

Regular Verbs Irregular Verbs
work → worked go → went
clean → cleaned buy → bought
start → started have → had
save → saved spend → spent
make → made

There is no single spelling rule for irregular verbs, so they must be learned little by little.

3. Affirmative

Use the Past Simple form directly.

I went to the bank.

She bought a new car.

He spent £200.

We had dinner at a restaurant.

They made a lot of money.

She became a manager.

4. Negative — Review

Even with irregular verbs, after didn't, use the base verb.

✅ I didn't go.

❌ I didn't went.

✅ She didn't buy the car.

❌ She didn't bought the car.

✅ They didn't spend much money.

❌ They didn't spent much money.

5. Questions — Review

DID + SUBJECT + BASE VERB...?

Did you go to the bank?

Did she buy a house?

Did he spend all his money?

Did they make a profit?

Did she become a manager?

Short answers

Did she buy the house? → Yes, she did.

Did she buy the house? → No, she didn't.

6. How to Talk About Money in the Past

A: How much did the car cost?

B: It cost £28,000.

A: Where did he get the money?

B: He sold his old car.

A: Did he save money too?

B: Yes, he did.

8. Important

After an exact number, we don't normally add -s to hundred, thousand or million.

✅ two hundred people

❌ two hundreds people

✅ five thousand pounds

❌ five thousands pounds

✅ three million dollars

❌ three millions dollars
