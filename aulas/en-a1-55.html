Canada — More Than Snow and Maple Syrup!
Did You Know These Things About Canada?
Canada is a very large country in North America. In fact, it is the second-largest country in the world, after Russia. It has the Atlantic Ocean to the east, the Pacific Ocean to the west and the Arctic Ocean to the north.
The capital of Canada is Ottawa, but some of its best-known cities are Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver.
Canada is famous for its beautiful nature. There are enormous forests, mountains, rivers and lakes. Winters are very cold in many parts of the country, and temperatures are often below 0°C. However, the weather is not the same everywhere. Around Vancouver, for example, winter is generally milder and rain is more common than snow.
But perhaps Canada's most famous symbol is the maple leaf. 🍁
The maple leaf has been a Canadian symbol for a very long time. It appears in the centre of the Canadian flag, and the leaf on the flag has exactly eleven points. The current Canadian flag was first raised on February 15th, 1965.
Maple trees are also used to produce maple syrup, a sweet syrup that people often eat with pancakes. Canada is the world's largest producer and exporter of maple products, and most Canadian production comes from Quebec.
So Canada isn't only about cold weather. It is a huge country with interesting cities, spectacular landscapes and some very famous traditions.

Section 2 of 5
Language Review

1. Present Simple

We use the Present Simple for facts.

Canada is in North America.

Canada has many lakes.

Many Canadians enjoy outdoor activities.

Maple syrup comes from maple trees.

2. Was / Were

The Canadian flag was different before 1965.

The maple leaf was already an important Canadian symbol before the current flag.

Was the current flag official in 1950?

→ No, it wasn't.

Was it first raised in 1965?

→ Yes, it was.

3. How + Adjective

HOW BIG

How big is Canada?

→ It's enormous. It's the second-largest country in the world.

HOW COLD

How cold is Canada in winter?

→ In many places, temperatures are often below 0°C.

HOW OLD

How old was the current Canadian flag in 2000?

→ It was 35 years old.

HOW FAR

How far is Canada from your country?

→ It's very far from my country.

4. Curiosity Challenge

What is the capital of Canada? → Ottawa.

Is Toronto the capital? → No, it isn't.

What is on the Canadian flag? → A maple leaf.

How many points does it have? → Eleven.

When was the current flag first raised? → In 1965.

Was the current flag used in 1950? → No, it wasn't.
