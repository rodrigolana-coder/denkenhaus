Why Do British People Drive on the Left?
In the United Kingdom, people drive on the left side of the road.
For many visitors, this is one of the first differences they notice.
But why do British people drive on the left?
The tradition is very old. Hundreds of years ago, many people travelled on horseback.
Most people were right-handed, so travelling on the left made it easier to keep the right hand free.
Later, when cars became popular, Britain continued using the left side of the road.
Today, people drive on the left in the United Kingdom, but also in countries such as Australia, New Zealand, Japan and India.
British cars are also different from cars in countries like Brazil, France and the United States.
In the UK, the driver sits on the right side of the car.
If you visit London, you can sometimes see the words LOOK RIGHT or LOOK LEFT painted on the road near pedestrian crossings.
They help tourists remember which direction the cars are coming from.
So, if you visit the UK, remember: look carefully before you cross the road!
