Grammar — Past Simple of TO BE

1. Affirmative — Review

SubjectVerb
Iwas
youwere
hewas
shewas
itwas
wewere
youwere
theywere

I was at school.

She was very young.

We were happy.

They were at home.

2. Negative — WASN'T / WEREN'T

Use wasn't with I / he / she / it.

I wasn't tired.

He wasn't at home.

She wasn't a teacher.

It wasn't expensive.

Use weren't with you / we / they.

You weren't late.

We weren't at school.

They weren't at home.

3. Questions — WAS...? / WERE...?

Put was/were before the subject.

Was she at school? → Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't.

Was it a good film? → Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.

Were you tired? → Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.

Were they at home? → Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.

4. Were you...? → I was...

Notice this difference:

Were you at home yesterday? → Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.

Not: ❌ Yes, I were.

5. Present × Past

Now: I am at home now. → Past: I was at home yesterday.

Now: She is a teacher. → Past: She was a student ten years ago.

Now: They are happy. → Past: They were happy when they were children.
