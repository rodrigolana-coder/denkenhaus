Grammar — Be Going To

BE GOING TO

We use be going to + verb to talk about future plans and intentions.

Affirmative

I

I am going to + verb

I'm going to travel.

I'm going to study.

I'm going to buy a new car.

He / She / It

is going to + verb

He's going to travel.

She's going to start university.

It's going to be difficult.

You / We / They

are going to + verb

You're going to visit London.

We're going to stay in a hotel.

They're going to move house.

Form

⚠️ The Verb After Going To

After going to, use the base form.

✅ I'm going to travel.

✅ She's going to buy a car.

✅ We're going to go to Canada.

❌ I'm going to travelled.

❌ She's going to bought a car.

Going To + Go

Yes, this structure is correct:

I'm going to go to Australia.

We're going to go to the beach.

In conversation, people may also simply say:

I'm going to Australia next month.

Both are possible.

Present × Past × Future

DRIVE

Present: I drive to work every day.

Past: I drove to work yesterday.

Future plan: I'm going to drive to work tomorrow.

SPEND

Present: I spend my holidays at home.

Past: I spent my holiday in France last year.

Future plan: I'm going to spend my holiday in Canada next year.

These are the only five new irregular verbs to memorise in Class 66.

FLY → FLEW

Present: I fly to London every year.

Past: I flew to London last year.

Future: I'm going to fly to London next month.

MEET → MET

Present: I meet my friends every Friday.

Past: I met them last Friday.

Future: I'm going to meet them tomorrow.

SPEND → SPENT

Present: I spend my holidays at home.

Past: I spent two weeks in Spain.

Future: I'm going to spend three weeks in Canada.

DRIVE → DROVE

Present: I drive to work.

Past: I drove to work yesterday.

Future: I'm going to drive to Manchester tomorrow.

SLEEP → SLEPT

Present: I sleep eight hours every night.

Past: I slept for ten hours last night.

Future: I'm going to sleep early tonight.
