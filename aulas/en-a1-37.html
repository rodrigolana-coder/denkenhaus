A: How often do you do the laundry? — B: Once a week.

Section 2 of 5
Grammar

1. Review — LIKE + -ING

Use like + verb + ing to talk about activities you enjoy.

I like cooking.

She likes reading.

We like travelling.

They like listening to music.

He likes taking photos.

2. Review — WANT + TO + VERB

Use want + to + verb to talk about what you want to do.

I want to cook dinner.

She wants to buy a laptop.

We want to watch a film.

They want to travel.

He wants to relax.

3. Review — Adverbs of frequency

FrequencyAdverb
100%always
90%usually
70%often
50%sometimes

0%never

I always make my bed.

She usually cooks dinner.

We often watch TV.

They sometimes clean the house.

I never vacuum the house.

4. Review — HOW OFTEN...?

Use How often...? to ask about frequency.

How often do you cook?

How often do you watch TV?

How often do you clean your room?

How often do you do the laundry?

Common answers

Every day. Every weekend. Once a week. Twice a week. Three times a week. Sometimes. Never.
