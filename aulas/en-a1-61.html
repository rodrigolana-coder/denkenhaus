Dialogues
Dialogue 1 — A Business Idea
Emma I had an idea for a small business yesterday.
Lucas Really? What was it?
Emma I want to make and sell T-shirts online.
Lucas That's a good idea. Why don't you make a website?
Emma I already did. I made a simple website last night.
Lucas Did you buy the T-shirts too?
Emma Yes. I bought ten.
Lucas Great! Let's see what happens.
Dialogue 2 — The First Customer
Anna Guess what! I got my first customer yesterday.
John That's great! What did they buy?
Anna They bought two T-shirts.
John How did they find your business?
Anna They went to my website.
John Did you make much money?
Anna Not much, but it's a start.
Dialogue 3 — Starting a Business
Sophie Did you always have your own business?
Daniel No. I had a normal job before this.
Sophie So, how did you start?
Daniel My friend and I had an idea. We went online and looked at different businesses.
Sophie What did you do next?
Daniel We made a business plan and bought some equipment.
Sophie And when did you get your first customer?
Daniel About two weeks later.

Grammar — Irregular Verbs: Set 1

1. Five Irregular Verbs

In this class, we only learn five new irregular verbs.

Present Past Português
go went ir
have had ter
get got conseguir / obter / receber
make made fazer / criar
buy bought comprar

2. Go → Went

I go to the bank every week.

I went to the bank yesterday.

3. Have → Had

I have a good idea.

I had a good idea yesterday.

4. Get → Got

I get messages every day.

I got a message this morning.

5. Make → Made

We make our products at home.

We made twenty products yesterday.

6. Buy → Bought

I usually buy things online.

I bought a new phone last week.

7. Questions and Negatives

In affirmative sentences, use the irregular past form.

She bought a computer.

They went to the bank.

We made a website.

But after did / didn't, use the base verb.

Did she buy a computer?

Did they go to the bank?

We didn't make a website.

He didn't get the job.

8. Making Suggestions

Let's + verb

Let's make a website.
