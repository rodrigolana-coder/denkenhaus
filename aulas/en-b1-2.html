Dialogue — How Do You Spend Your Free Time?
SophieWhat do you usually do in your free time?
BenIt depends. During the week, I normally stay in, but I love going out at the weekend.
SophieWhat kind of places do you like going to?
BenI enjoy going to the cinema and trying new restaurants. I also like going to concerts when I can.
SophieSo you’re quite a culture vulture!
BenMaybe! But I’m not really into museums or art galleries. What about you?
SophieI actually love visiting exhibitions and museums, but I hate going to crowded clubs.
BenReally? I don’t mind clubs, but I prefer meeting friends somewhere we can talk.
SophieDo you do much sport in your free time?
BenI sometimes go for a run, but I’m not very sporty. I’d rather go for a walk or stay home and watch a film.
SophieThat sounds like a good weekend to me.
BenWhat do you normally do on Sundays?
SophieI usually have a quiet day. I read, listen to music and sometimes go for a walk.
BenSame here. I like going out, but I also need some time to relax before Monday.

Talking about likes and dislikes

I love going to concerts.
Eu adoro ir a shows.

I enjoy visiting museums.
Eu gosto de visitar museus.

I like spending time with my friends.
Eu gosto de passar tempo com meus amigos.

I don’t mind staying at home.
Eu não me importo de ficar em casa.

I don’t really like going clubbing.
Eu não gosto muito de ir a baladas.

I hate getting up early at the weekend.
Eu odeio levantar cedo no fim de semana.

Remember

After like, love, enjoy, don’t mind and hate, we can use the -ing form when talking about activities.

I like reading.
She loves going out.
We enjoy watching films.
He hates getting up early.

Types of people

a party animal — someone who loves parties and going out
a workaholic — someone who spends a lot of time working
a couch potato — someone who spends a lot of time sitting and watching TV
a culture vulture — someone who is very interested in art, museums, theatre and exhibitions
