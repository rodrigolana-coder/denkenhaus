Dialogue 1 — A Birthday Dinner
EmmaHow was your birthday?
LucasIt was great! My family came to my house for dinner.
EmmaWhat did you eat?
LucasWe ate pizza and salad.
EmmaAnd what did you drink?
LucasI drank orange juice, but my parents had tea.
EmmaDid you get any presents?
LucasYes! My sister gave me a new watch.
Dialogue 2 — A Special Present
AnnaWhat did you give your mother for her birthday?
JohnI took her to a nice restaurant.
AnnaThat's a great present! Why did you choose that restaurant?
JohnBecause she went there when she was younger and really loved it.
AnnaWhat did you eat?
JohnWe ate Italian food.
AnnaDid she like the surprise?
JohnYes. She said it was wonderful.
Dialogue 3 — Visiting an Old Friend
SophieWhat did you do last weekend?
DanielI went to Manchester and saw an old friend.
SophieReally? How long did you stay there?
DanielTwo days.
SophieDid you take him anything?
DanielYes. I bought him some chocolates.
SophieWhy chocolates?
DanielBecause I knew they were his favourite.
SophieThat's really kind.

Grammar — Irregular Verbs: Set 4

⭐ Irregular Verbs — Set 4

We already have 15 irregular verbs in our active list. Now we add only five new verbs.

PresentPastPortuguês
comecamevir
seesawver
taketookpegar / levar
eatatecomer

These are the five new irregular verbs to memorise in Class 64.

COME → CAME

Present: My friends come to my house every weekend.

Past: My friends came to my house yesterday.

My family came to my birthday dinner.

Who came to the party?

SEE → SAW

Present: I see my friends every week.

Past: I saw my friends last Saturday.

Daniel saw an old friend.

I saw my teacher yesterday.

TAKE → TOOK

Present: I take the bus to work.

Past: I took the bus yesterday.

It can also mean levar:

I took my mother to a restaurant.

She took a present to the party.

EAT → ATE

Present: We eat dinner at seven.

Past: We ate dinner at eight yesterday.

We ate pizza.

She ate at a restaurant.

DRINK → DRANK

Present: I drink coffee every morning.

Past: I drank coffee this morning.

They drank orange juice.

He drank tea after dinner.

Did / Didn't — Review

Remember that after did or didn't, we use the base form.

Affirmative

They came to my house.

Question

Did they come to your house?

Negative

They didn't come to my house.

She ate pizza.

Did she eat pizza?

She didn't eat pizza.

He drank coffee.

Did he drink coffee?

He didn't drink coffee.

Why / Because — Review

WHY

Use Why...? to ask for a reason.

Why did you buy her chocolates?

Why did you take her to that restaurant?

BECAUSE

Use because to give the reason.

Because she loves chocolates.

Because it was her birthday.

Translation

Translate from Portuguese into English.

1. Minha família veio à minha casa ontem.
My family came to my house yesterday.
2. Eu vi um amigo antigo no fim de semana passado.
I saw an old friend last weekend.
3. Nós levamos nossa mãe a um restaurante.
We took our mother to a restaurant.
4. Eles comeram pizza no jantar.
They ate pizza for dinner.
5. Eu bebi café esta manhã.
I drank coffee this morning.
6. Quem veio à sua festa?
Who came to your party?
7. Você viu seus amigos no fim de semana?

8. O que vocês comeram no restaurante?

9. Por que você levou sua mãe para jantar?

10. Porque era o aniversário dela.
Because it was her birthday.
11. Ela ganhou um presente e disse que adorou.
She got a present and said she loved it.
12. Nós fomos ao restaurante e compramos um presente depois.
We went to the restaurant and bought a present afterwards.
