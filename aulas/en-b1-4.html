Dialogue — What Are You Doing?
EmmaHi, Jake. What are you doing at the moment?
JakeI’m sitting in a café and waiting for a friend. What about you?
EmmaI’m working from home today, but I’m taking a short break now.
JakeAre you working on anything interesting at the moment?
EmmaYes. I’m helping with a new project, so I’m spending a lot of time in online meetings this week.
JakeThat sounds busy. Are you enjoying it?
EmmaI am, actually. I’m learning a lot, but I’m also working longer hours than usual.
JakeI know the feeling. I’m studying for an exam at the moment.
EmmaReally? What are you studying?
JakeSpanish. I’m trying to improve my speaking because I’m travelling to Madrid next month.
EmmaThat’s exciting! Are you studying every day?
JakeAlmost. Right now, I’m trying to practise for at least thirty minutes a day.
EmmaWell, you aren’t practising now!
JakeTrue! At the moment, I’m drinking coffee and waiting for someone who is twenty minutes late.

Present Continuous — actions happening now

I’m drinking coffee right now.
Eu estou tomando café agora.

She’s talking on the phone.
Ela está falando ao telefone.

They’re waiting outside.
Eles estão esperando lá fora.

Temporary situations around now

We also use the Present Continuous for temporary situations happening around now, even if they are not happening at this exact second.

I’m studying Spanish at the moment.
Estou estudando espanhol no momento.

She’s working on a new project this month.
Ela está trabalhando em um novo projeto este mês.

We’re spending more time at home these days.
Estamos passando mais tempo em casa ultimamente.

Structure

Affirmative: I am working. He is studying. They are waiting.

Negative: I am not working. She isn’t studying. They aren’t waiting.

Questions: Are you working? Is she studying? What are they doing?
