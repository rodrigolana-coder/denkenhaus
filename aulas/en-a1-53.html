Grammar Review — HOW WAS/WERE + HOW + ADJECTIVE

1. How was / How were...?

Use How was...? to ask about a past experience or singular thing.

How was your childhood? → It was great.

How was your school? → It was really nice.

Use How were...? with plural nouns.

How were your teachers? → They were friendly.

How were your school years? → They were great.

2. How + adjective

Remember the structure: HOW + ADJECTIVE + WAS/WERE + SUBJECT?

How old were you in 2010? → I was ten years old.

How old was your teacher? → He was about forty.

How big was your school? → It was quite big.

How big was your hometown? → It was very small.

How far was your school from your house? → It was about two kilometres away.

How far was your hometown from the capital? → It was about fifty kilometres away.

How long was your school day? → It was about six hours.

How long were your holidays? → They were two months.

How tall was your father when he was young? → He was about 1.80 metres tall.

How tall was the building? → It was about thirty metres tall.

How wide was the river? → It was about fifty metres wide.

3. Quick review

How old...? → age

How big...? → size

How far...? → distance

How long...? → duration / length

How tall...? → height

How wide...? → width

4. How was...? × How big was...?

Be careful — general opinion vs specific information.

General: How was your school? → It was great.

Specific: How big was your school? → It was very big. There were 800 students.

General: How was your holiday? → It was fantastic.

Duration: How long was your holiday? → It was two weeks.
