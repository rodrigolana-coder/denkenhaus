A: Can your brother drive? — B: No, he can’t.

A: When is your birthday? — B: It’s on May fifteenth.

A: When is the concert? — B: It’s on August twenty-second.

A: What time does it start? — B: At eight o’clock.

Grammar Review

1. CAN / CAN’T

Affirmative — Subject + can + verb

I can swim. She can drive. They can speak English.

Negative — Subject + can’t + verb

I can’t sing. He can’t cook. We can’t play the piano.

Questions — Can + subject + verb?

Can you drive? Can she dance? Can they speak French?

Short answers

Yes, I can. / No, I can’t. / Yes, she can. / No, she can’t.

📌 After can/can’t, use the verb without to. I can swim. ✅ — I can to swim. ❌

3. IN / ON / AT

IN — months, years, parts of the day

in July, in 2026, in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening

ON — days and dates

on Monday, on Saturday, on May 5th, on August 22nd

AT — times

at seven o’clock, at 8:30, at noon, at midnight

Compare: My birthday is in May. My birthday is on May 15th. My birthday party starts at 8:00.
