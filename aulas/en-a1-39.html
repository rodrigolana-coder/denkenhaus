How to... talk about your free time

A: What do you usually do in your free time? — B: I usually play tennis.

A: How often do you go cycling? — B: Twice a week.

A: Are you playing football now? — B: Yes, I am.

A: Do you like doing puzzles? — B: Yes, I do.

Section 2 of 5
Grammar

1. Review — Present Simple

Use the Present Simple for habits and routines.

I play chess every weekend.

She goes cycling on Saturdays.

They usually play football.

We often go shopping.

2. Review — Present Continuous

Use the Present Continuous for actions happening now.

I’m playing chess now.

She’s cycling.

They’re playing football.

We’re going to the gym.

3. Present Simple × Present Continuous

Present SimplePresent Continuous
I play football every Saturday.I’m playing football now.
She goes cycling twice a week.She’s cycling now.
We usually go shopping.We’re shopping now.
They play chess after school.They’re playing chess now.

4. Time expressions

Present Simple

every day, every weekend, usually, often, sometimes, never, twice a week

Present Continuous

now, right now, at the moment, today, this afternoon, tonight
