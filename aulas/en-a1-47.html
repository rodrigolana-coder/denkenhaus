Section 2 of 5
Grammar

1. Saying years — 1900–1999

We normally divide the year into two parts.

1935 → nineteen thirty-five

1946 → nineteen forty-six

1969 → nineteen sixty-nine

1981 → nineteen eighty-one

1995 → nineteen ninety-five

2. Saying years — 2000–2009

2000 → two thousand

2001 → two thousand and one

2002 → two thousand and two

2006 → two thousand and six

2009 → two thousand and nine

3. Saying years — 2010 onwards

We can commonly say:

2010 → twenty ten

2015 → twenty fifteen

2020 → twenty twenty

2026 → twenty twenty-six

4. Year × date

Be careful with the difference:

1969 → nineteen sixty-nine → a year

July 20th → July twentieth → a date

July 20th, 1969 → July twentieth, nineteen sixty-nine

5. Review — IN / ON / AT

IN + year

in 1935, in 1969, in 2001, in 2026

The first man walked on the Moon in 1969.

ON + date

on May 5th, on July 20th, on October 31st

My birthday is on May 5th.

AT + time

at 7:00, at 8:30, at noon

The film starts at 8:30.

6. How to... ask about years & dates

A: What year were you born? — B: I was born in 1998.

A: When is your birthday? — B: It’s on June twenty-first.

A: When did the first man walk on the Moon? — B: In 1969.

📌 "I was born..." and "When did...?" appear here as useful expressions. The Past Simple itself will be studied later.
