Dialogue 1 — A Birthday Present
EmmaWhat did you get for your birthday?
LucasMy parents gave me a new watch.
EmmaThat's a nice present! Why did they give you a watch?
LucasBecause I needed a new one.
EmmaDid you like it?
LucasYes, I loved it. I wore it to work yesterday.
EmmaWhere did they buy it?
LucasThey bought it online.

AnnaI need to buy a present for my sister.
JohnWhy?
AnnaBecause it's her birthday on Saturday.
JohnWhat does she like?
AnnaShe loves reading, so I thought about buying her a book.
JohnThat's a good idea. What did you give her last year?
AnnaI gave her some jewellery, but she lost it!
JohnOh no! Maybe a book is safer.
Dialogue 3 — A Surprise Gift
SophieLook what Daniel gave me!
EmmaWow! Are those concert tickets?
SophieYes! He gave them to me yesterday.
EmmaWhy did he buy you concert tickets?
SophieBecause he knew I really wanted to see this band.
EmmaThat's very kind. What did you say?
SophieI said, “Thank you!” Then I gave him a big hug.

Grammar — Why & Because

WHY...?

We use why to ask for a reason.

Why do you want a new phone?

Why does she want chocolates?

In the past:

Why did you buy that present?

Why did he give her flowers?

Why did they go to the shop?

BECAUSE

We use because to give the reason.

Why did you buy her a book?
→ Because she loves reading.

Why did he give her concert tickets?
→ Because she wanted to see the band.

Why did you buy a new phone?
→ Because my old phone was broken.

⚠️ DID + BASE VERB — Review

The irregular form is used in affirmative sentences:

She gave me a present.

But after did / didn't, use the base form:

Did she give you a present?

She didn't give me a present.

❌ Did she gave you a present?
