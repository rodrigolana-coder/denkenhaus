Dialogue — A Typical Day
EmmaWhat time do you usually get up during the week?
DanielMy alarm goes off at half past six, but I normally stay in bed for another ten minutes.
EmmaThat’s early. What do you do first?
DanielI have a shower, make some coffee and check my emails while I’m having breakfast.
EmmaHow do you get to work?
DanielI usually take the bus. It takes about thirty minutes, so I listen to music on the way.
EmmaDo you finish work late?
DanielNot usually, but occasionally I have to stay late when we’re busy.
EmmaWhat do you normally do in the evening?
DanielI try to exercise a few times a week. Otherwise, I just stay in, make dinner and watch TV.
EmmaAnd what about weekends?
DanielI normally get up later and meet friends or spend time with my family.
EmmaWhat’s your favourite part of the week?
DanielDefinitely Friday evening. I love knowing I don’t have to get up early the next morning.

Translate into English
1. Meu despertador normalmente toca às seis e meia.

2. Às vezes eu fico na cama por mais dez minutos.

3. Eu verifico meus e-mails enquanto tomo café da manhã.
I check my emails while I’m having breakfast.
4. Como você normalmente vai para o trabalho?

5. Eu geralmente pego o ônibus.
I usually take the bus.
6. Leva cerca de trinta minutos para chegar ao trabalho.

7. Eu gosto de ouvir música no caminho.

8. Ocasionalmente, eu preciso ficar no trabalho até mais tarde.

9. Eu tento fazer exercício algumas vezes por semana.
