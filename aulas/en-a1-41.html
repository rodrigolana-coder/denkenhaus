How to... make suggestions

A: Shall we go to the cinema? — B: That’s a great idea!

A: Why don’t we go for a walk? — B: I’d love to.

A: Let’s play tennis. — B: Sounds good!

A: Shall we have dinner together? — B: Yes, let’s.
