Section 2 of 5
Grammar

1. Present Continuous

We use the Present Continuous to talk about actions happening now, at this moment.

I’m studying now.

She’s cooking dinner.

They’re watching TV.

We’re cleaning the house.

2. Form

SubjectVerb
Iam + verb-ing
Youare + verb-ing
Heis + verb-ing
Sheis + verb-ing
Itis + verb-ing
Weare + verb-ing
Theyare + verb-ing

I’m reading. You’re listening. He’s working. She’s cooking. It’s raining. We’re studying. They’re playing football.

3. Negative

I’m not sleeping.

You aren’t studying.

He isn’t working.

She isn’t watching TV.

We aren’t cooking.

They aren’t playing.

4. Questions

What are you doing?

What is he doing?

What is she doing?

Are they studying?

Is he working?

Is she reading?

Short answers

Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.

Yes, he is. / No, he isn’t.

Yes, they are. / No, they aren’t.

5. Present Simple × Present Continuous

Present Simple — routines, habits and facts.

I work every day.

She studies English.

They play football on Saturdays.

Present Continuous — what is happening now.

I’m working now.

She’s studying at the moment.

They’re playing football now.

Present SimplePresent Continuous
I work every day.I’m working now.
She reads every evening.She’s reading now.
We usually eat at home.We’re eating at a restaurant.
They play football on Saturdays.They’re playing football now.
