Dialogue 1 — My First Part-Time Job
EmmaDid you work when you were a teenager?
LucasYes, I did. I worked part-time in a cinema.
EmmaWhat did you do there?
LucasI cleaned the cinema and helped customers.
EmmaDid you like the job?
LucasYes, I did. I really liked it.
EmmaDid you earn much money?
LucasNo, I didn’t, but I saved some of it.
Dialogue 2 — Money When I Was Younger
AnnaWere you careful with money when you were younger?
JohnNo, I wasn’t. I was terrible with money!
AnnaReally? Did you spend a lot?
JohnYes, I did. I spent most of my money on clothes and computer games.
AnnaDid you save anything?
JohnNo, I didn’t.
AnnaAnd what about now?
JohnI’m much more careful with money now.
Dialogue 3 — A Weekend Job
SophieWhat was your first job?
DanielI washed cars at the weekend.
SophieDid you work alone?
DanielNo, I didn’t. I worked with my brother.
SophieHow much money did you earn?
DanielNot much, but it was enough for me.
SophieWhat did you do with the money?
DanielI saved some and spent some.

2. Spelling Review

Most verbs → + ED

work → worked

clean → cleaned

Verb ending in -E → + D

live → lived

love → loved

Consonant + Y → IED

study → studied

tidy → tidied

3. Pronunciation — -ED

/t/

worked · washed · helped · liked

/d/

cleaned · lived · loved · listened

/ɪd/

started · needed · hated

4. Negative

SUBJECT + DIDN’T + BASE VERB

I didn’t work yesterday.

She didn’t save any money.

They didn’t play the lottery.

We didn’t watch TV.

Remember: after didn’t, use the base verb.

I didn’t worked. ❌

I didn’t work. ✅

She didn’t saved money. ❌

She didn’t save money. ✅

5. Questions

DID + SUBJECT + BASE VERB...?

Did you work yesterday?

Did she save money?

Did they play the lottery?

Did he like his job?

Short answers

Did you work? → Yes, I did.

Did you work? → No, I didn’t.

6. WH- Questions

WH-WORD + DID + SUBJECT + BASE VERB?

Where did you work?

When did you start?

Why did you leave?

What did you buy?

How much did you earn?

What did you spend your money on?

7. Did × Was / Were

Action verb → DID

Did you work when you were sixteen?

Did she save money?

Where did they live?

Verb TO BE → WAS / WERE

Were you happy at your first job?

Was the job difficult?

Were your parents careful with money?

ActionTo be
Did you work in a supermarket?Were you a supermarket employee?
Yes, I did.Yes, I was.
