Section 2 of 5
Grammar — Questions with HOW

1. How was / How were...?

Use How was...? with singular nouns.

How was your weekend?

How was your trip?

How was the hotel?

How was the food?

Use How were...? with plural nouns.

How were your holidays?

How were your classes?

How were your friends?

How were the restaurants?

How was your weekend? → It was great.

How were your holidays? → They were fantastic.

2. How + adjective

We can use How + adjective to ask for specific information.

How old is your brother? → He's twenty-five.

How old were you in 2015? → I was eighteen.

How long was your holiday? → It was two weeks.

How long were you in London? → I was there for five days.

How far is the airport? → It's about ten kilometres from here.

How far was the hotel from the city centre? → It was about two kilometres.

How big is your house? → It's quite small.

How big was the hotel? → It was very big.

How tall is your brother? → He's 1.80 metres tall.

How tall was the building? → It was about 100 metres tall.

How wide is the table? → It's about one metre wide.

3. Important — structure

HOW + ADJECTIVE + TO BE + SUBJECT?

How old is she?

How long was your holiday?

How far is the airport?

How big was the hotel?

How tall is that building?

4. How was...? × What was... like?

These questions can have a very similar meaning.

How was your weekend?

What was your weekend like?

It was great. / It was relaxing. / It was terrible. / It was really fun.

5. Review — WAS / WERE

How was your trip? → It was fantastic.

Was the hotel expensive? → No, it wasn't.

How were your holidays? → They were great.

Were the restaurants good? → Yes, they were.
