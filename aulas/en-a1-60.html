🇺🇸 Why Is Route 66 So Famous?

Route 66 is one of the most famous roads in the United States.

It was created in 1926 and originally connected Chicago, Illinois, to Santa Monica, California. The road crossed several states and became an important route for people travelling across the country.

For many Americans, Route 66 became a symbol of freedom, travel and adventure.

In the past, many families used Route 66 when they moved west looking for work and a better life. Later, tourists travelled along the road to visit small towns, diners, motels and famous roadside attractions.

Route 66 was officially removed from the U.S. Highway System in 1985, but it did not disappear. Many parts of the old road still exist today.

People can still drive on sections of historic Route 66, visit old petrol stations, eat in traditional diners and take photos of famous signs.

The road also appeared in songs, films and television programmes, which helped make it even more famous around the world.

Today, Route 66 is not just a road. It is an important part of American history and popular culture.

Irregular Past Simple

Some familiar irregular verbs also appear naturally when we talk about history.

go → went

have → had

become → became

People went west.

Tourists had long journeys.

The road became famous.
