Section 2 of 5
Grammar

1. LIKE + -ING

After like, we normally use the -ing form of the verb when talking about activities.

I like watching TV.

You like listening to music.

We like playing football.

They like taking photos.

She likes reading books.

He likes surfing the Internet.

2. WANT + TO + VERB

After want, we use to + infinitive.

I want to buy a new phone.

You want to travel.

We want to watch a film.

They want to play football.

She wants to study English.

He wants to take photos.

3. Questions

Do you like watching TV?

Do you like listening to music?

Do you want to go to the cinema?

What do you want to buy?

4. Review — Present Simple

I like music.

She works in a hospital.

They live in London.

We usually study at night.
