The Holiday of a Lifetime

Dialogue 1 — Beach or Mountains?

Emma: What kind of holiday do you prefer?

Lucas: I usually prefer the beach, but this year I'm going to do something different.

Emma: Really? Where are you going to go?

Lucas: I'm going to the mountains in Switzerland.

Emma: That sounds amazing! Have you ever been there before?

Lucas: No. Last year I went to Spain and swam in the sea every day.

Emma: So why did you choose the mountains this time?

Lucas: Because I want a completely different experience.

Dialogue 2 — Camping Holiday

Anna: What are you going to do during your holiday?

John: We're going camping for a week.

Anna: Where are you going to stay?

John: In a campsite near a lake.

Anna: Are you going to cook your own food?

John: Yes. Last time we went camping, we brought all our food with us.

Anna: Did you enjoy it?

John: Yes! We sat by the lake every evening and had dinner together.

Dialogue 3 — Planning the Holiday

Sophie: Have you decided where you're going this summer?

Daniel: Yes. We're going to Italy.

Sophie: Nice! What are you going to do there?

Daniel: We're going to spend a few days in Rome and then travel around the country.

Sophie: How are you going to travel?

Daniel: By train. We found some cheap tickets online.

Sophie: What are you most excited about?

Daniel: The food! Last time I was in Italy, I ate incredible pasta and drank great coffee.

Grammar — Going to & Irregular Verbs

Future plans

Affirmative: I'm going to travel. / We're going to visit Rome.

Negative: I'm not going to work. / We aren't going to drive.

Questions: Are you going to travel? / Where are you going to stay?

Past holiday × Future holiday

Last year, I went to Spain. This year, I'm going to go to Italy.

We drove to France. We're going to fly to France.
