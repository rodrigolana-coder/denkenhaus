🇦🇺 Why Is Australia So Different?

Australia is one of the most unusual countries in the English-speaking world.

It is both a country and a continent, and it is famous for animals that do not naturally live in many other places.

One of the best-known Australian animals is the kangaroo. Kangaroos are very important in Australian culture and even appear on the country's coat of arms.

Australia is also famous for the koala. Koalas spend a lot of time in trees and usually eat eucalyptus leaves.

Another famous Australian animal is the platypus. It looks very unusual because it has a bill like a duck, but it is a mammal.

Australia also has some of the most famous natural places in the world. The Great Barrier Reef is the largest coral reef system on Earth, and many tourists visit it to see colourful fish, coral and other marine animals.

The country is also very large. Many Australians live in cities near the coast, while the centre of the country is much more isolated and dry.

Australia is in the Southern Hemisphere, so its seasons are opposite to countries in Europe and North America. When it is winter in the UK, it is summer in Australia.

So, Australia is not only famous for beaches and kangaroos. It is a country with unique wildlife, enormous landscapes and a very different geography.

Language Review

Present Simple

Kangaroos live in Australia.

Koalas eat eucalyptus leaves.

Many Australians live near the coast.

Australia has unique animals.

CAN

Tourists can see colourful fish at the Great Barrier Reef.

Kangaroos can jump very far.

THERE IS / THERE ARE

There are many unusual animals in Australia.

There is a lot of dry land in the centre of the country.

HOW + ADJECTIVE

How big is Australia?

→ It's enormous.

How far is Australia from Brazil?

→ It's very far.

How hot is Australia in summer?

→ In many places, it can be very hot.
