Examples

He's rich. → He has a lot of money.

She's broke. → She doesn't have any money at the moment.

He's generous. → He likes giving things or money to other people.

She's mean. → She doesn't like spending money on other people.

He's careful with money. → He thinks before he spends.

She's careless with money. → She spends without thinking carefully.

Rags to riches

Rags to riches is an expression used for someone who starts life with very little money and later becomes rich or successful.

He went from rags to riches.

Grammar — Past Simple: Negatives & Questions

1. Negative

In Class 56, we learned the affirmative: I worked in a cinema.

To make the negative, use: SUBJECT + DIDN'T + BASE VERB.

I didn't work yesterday.

She didn't save her money.

We didn't watch TV.

They didn't play the lottery.

He didn't earn much money.

Important: after didn't, the verb returns to its base form.

❌ I didn't worked. ✅ I didn't work.

❌ She didn't saved money. ✅ She didn't save money.

2. Questions

Use: DID + SUBJECT + BASE VERB...?

Did you work yesterday?

Did she save her money?

Did they play the lottery?

Did he earn a lot?

Did you like your first job?

❌ Did you worked there? ✅ Did you work there?

3. Short answers

Did you save money? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

Did she work there? → Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.

Did they play the lottery? → Yes, they did. / No, they didn't.

4. Wh- questions

QUESTION WORD + DID + SUBJECT + VERB?

Where did you work?

What did you buy?

When did she start?

Why did he leave?

How much money did you earn?

What did you spend your money on?

5. Did × Was/Were

We don't use did with was/were.

❌ Did you be poor? ✅ Were you poor?

Was your first job difficult?

Did you work when you were a teenager?

Did you save money?

6. Affirmative × Negative × Question

WORK: I worked yesterday. / I didn't work yesterday. / Did you work yesterday?

SAVE: She saved some money. / She didn't save any money. / Did she save any money?

PLAY: They played the lottery. / They didn't play the lottery. / Did they play the lottery?
