Dialogue — A Busy Week
LauraWhat are your weekdays usually like?
MarkPretty busy. I normally get up at six because I start work at half past seven.
LauraThat’s early! Do you have breakfast at home?
MarkUsually, yes. My wife and I have breakfast together, but the children often eat later.
LauraWhat does your wife do during the day?
MarkShe works from home three days a week, so she usually takes the children to school.
LauraAnd what happens on the other two days?
MarkI take them before work. We try to share everything as much as possible.
LauraDo you ever have time to exercise during the week?
MarkI go to the gym twice a week, usually before dinner. My wife prefers going for a run in the mornings.
LauraWhat do you normally do in the evenings?
MarkWe have dinner together and talk about our day. Afterwards, the children do their homework and we usually relax.
LauraDo you go out much from Monday to Friday?
MarkHardly ever. By Friday evening, we’re normally too tired to do anything!
