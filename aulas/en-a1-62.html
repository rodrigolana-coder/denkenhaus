LucasI got a new job!
EmmaThat's great! When did you find out?

These are the five new verbs to memorise in Class 62.

I said hello to my neighbours every morning. → I said hello to them yesterday. — Sophie said yes.

My friend tells me everything. → My friend told me the news yesterday.

SAY + something (She said yes.) × TELL + someone + something (She told me the news.)

I leave work at six. → I left work at six yesterday. — Lucas left his old job last week.

I usually find jobs online. → I found my last job online. — How did you find your new flat?

I hear a lot of interesting stories at work. → I heard the news yesterday. — I heard you moved house.

3. Sets 1 + 2 together

We now have 10 irregular verbs available.

go → went · have → had · get → got · make → made · buy → bought

say → said · tell → told · leave → left · find → found · hear → heard

The five verbs from Class 61 continue appearing naturally instead of being abandoned when we learn the new set.

4. Questions & negatives — review

Remember: after did / didn't, we use the base form.

She left her job. → Did she leave her job? → She didn't leave her job.

He found a new flat. → Did he find a new flat? → He didn't find a new flat.

She told me the news. → Did she tell you the news? → She didn't tell me the news.

5. How to... talk about life changes

A: What happened? — B: I got a new job.

A: Really? When did that happen? — B: Last month.

A: Did you leave your old job? — B: Yes, I did.

A: I heard you moved house. — B: Yes, I did.

A: How did you find your new flat? — B: A friend told me about it.
