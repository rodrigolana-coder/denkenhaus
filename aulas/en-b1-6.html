Dialogue — What Kind of Music Are You Into?
LucyWhat kind of music are you into?
TomMostly rock and indie, but I listen to a bit of everything. What about you?
LucyI’m really into soul and jazz at the moment. I love music with strong vocals.
TomDo you have a favourite artist?
LucyProbably Amy Winehouse. I never get tired of listening to her songs.
TomI know what you mean. There are some albums I’ve listened to hundreds of times.
LucyDo you ever go to concerts?
TomWhenever I can. I saw my favourite band live last year, and they were brilliant.
LucyI love live music too. The atmosphere is completely different from listening at home.
TomDefinitely. Do you play any instruments?
LucyI can play the piano a little, but I’m not very good at it. How about you?
TomI used to play the guitar, but I haven’t played for years.
LucyYou should start again!
TomMaybe I will. Talking about music is making me miss it!

Translate into English
1. Que tipo de música você gosta?
What kind of music are you into?
2. Eu ouço um pouco de tudo.
I listen to a bit of everything.
3. No momento, estou gostando muito de jazz.
At the moment, I’m really into jazz.
4. Quem é seu artista favorito?
Who is your favourite artist?
5. Eu nunca me canso de ouvir essa música.
I never get tired of listening to this song.
6. Essa é uma das minhas bandas favoritas.
This is one of my favourite bands.
7. Eu já ouvi esse álbum centenas de vezes.
I’ve listened to this album hundreds of times.
8. Você costuma ir a shows?
Do you often go to concerts?
9. Eu vi minha banda favorita ao vivo no ano passado.
I saw my favourite band live last year.
10. Eu adoro a atmosfera dos shows ao vivo.
I love the atmosphere at live concerts.
11. Você sabe tocar algum instrumento?
Can you play any instruments?
12. Eu sei tocar um pouco de piano, mas não sou muito bom.
I can play the piano a little, but I’m not very good at it.
13. Eu costumava tocar guitarra quando era mais jovem.
I used to play the guitar when I was younger.
14. Faz anos que eu não toco.
I haven’t played for years.
15. A letra dessa música é muito bonita.
The lyrics to this song are beautiful.
